# Final parity audit: old customer app (`apps/platform`) against `apps/customer`

Audit date: 2026-11-03, about 90 minutes after the cutover of app.brandbasket.in to the new app. Read-only: nothing was run, built, changed or deleted, and production was touched only by six public HEAD requests (section 5). Code is `origin/development` at `f4248a6f`.

## 1. Result

**Not yet safe to delete the old customer pages.** About 430 behaviours and addresses were compared. Almost all are KEPT or MOVED, and most server modules were diffed against their ports and are straight copies. The gaps below are real, and three of them are live now:

1. **Quote "Accept and pay" dead-ends on Razorpay** (money, G-1). The customer is sent to Plan & billing instead of the payment window, so the work does not start until they find the invoice and pay it there.
2. **Every customer email shows a broken logo** (G-2). The PNG lives only in `apps/platform/public`. Live check: `https://app.brandbasket.in/email/email-logo.png` returns 404.
3. **The new app is blind to its own errors** (G-3). Browser capture, the staff "App errors" inbox, the system-error email and the Teams billing-error alerts are not wired in `apps/customer`. Only Sentry is, and the browser DSN may not be in the build.

None of the gaps is a reason to roll back. Each is small, and all are fixed in the new app or in config, never by keeping old pages. After the "Must fix before deleting" items (section 3, G-1 to G-6) close, section 4 lists what can go.

## 2. How the audit was done, and what it cannot see

- Enumerated from the OLD code: every `page.tsx` and `route.ts` (including the 26 under `app/api`), server action exports, `packages/email` templates and link builders, `next.config.ts` redirects, `proxy.ts`, systemd units and `apps/platform/scripts`, env variables, rate limits, guards, idempotency keys, conversion and consent events, Sentry hooks. Each was matched to its counterpart in `apps/customer`, in a shared package, or in the platform routes that nginx keeps (`deploy/vps/nginx/brandbasket-split-platform-paths.conf.example`).
- Five independent readers (Sonnet sub-agents) each took a slice and wrote a table with file:line evidence: routes (83 rows), money and account (73), product features (101), APIs/jobs/env/infra (79), email links and tracking (98). Their full tables are the appendices below.
- **I re-read the code behind every claim in the "verified" column of section 3** and checked the live app host with HEAD requests. Claims marked "reader" were not independently re-read by me.
- `PARITY.md`, plan 61, `E2E-REVIEW.md` and `COPY-TABLE.md` were used only as leads. `E2E-REVIEW.md` is stale on E2E-01 and E2E-05 (both fixed in code, per the money reader). `PARITY.md` lists `/robots.txt` and `/.well-known/security.txt` as needed, and neither was built.
- `apps/customer/src/app/old-paths.test.ts` only proves a route file exists. It does not check redirect targets, query parameters, methods or headers, and it has no entry for robots, security.txt or the email logo. Treat it as weak evidence.
- Cannot be seen from the repo: the live nginx config (the cutover and split files are labelled NOT APPLIED), the live env files, whether timers are enabled, the marketing website repo, Cal.com event settings, production row counts. These are marked UNVERIFIED in the appendices.

#### Surprises

- S-1. Staff Teams alerts for new requests and customer replies are gone (F-042, F-045). Only preview feedback keeps its Teams card (website/alerts.ts:81). Easy to miss because the email still arrives.
- S-2. The staff-facing "native error inbox" and system-error alerts get nothing from the customer app (F-094 to F-096). Sentry is the only sink. `startBillingErrorAlerts` in platform/instrumentation.ts is the billing reader's, but it also no longer sees customer-app billing errors.
- S-3. The Website page lost its history surfaces: version list with outcomes, "Your changes", "Recent changes", "Change it yourself" (F-008 to F-011). The data is still written (`project_feedback_tickets`, `project_preview_versions`) and no longer read; only the newest preview version is read.
- S-4. A user who is both customer and affiliate has no sidebar route to /affiliate (F-101). Blog also has no sidebar link before launch (F-086).
- S-5. `businesses.kind = 'listing'` accounts: old guard sent them to /details or the membership page. New guards have no kind check, so such accounts would see the full dashboard (F-039). I could not see production to know whether any exist.
- S-6. Support attachments: a closing account can still fetch its screenshots and a signed-out request gets a redirect not a 401 (F-052).
- S-7. Customers who paid before the cutover with an incomplete checklist have no "Send" path (F-025). The kickoff job starts the build regardless of the checklist, so nothing blocks the build, but the customer-visible "sent" state (`onboardingSubmittedAt`) is never set for them.
- S-8. New rules that did not exist before (not regressions, but a behaviour change a tester will trip on): checklist "Your business" needs a phone or an email (F-021); domain checks capped at 20 per hour (F-015); blog uploads have an hourly budget and file-magic checks (F-075); many writes now refuse viewers and require a running paid period (F-001, F-016, F-031, F-036, F-064).
- S-9. Plan 61:103 says the domain page has "all 8 current states"; the code in both apps has 9 (PARITY.md:15 already notes it). Harmless; both apps agree.
- S-10. The customer business/actions.ts comment at line 171 says brand uploads go to the old app's route; they do not (customer has its own `/api/brand-assets`, and the nginx platform split does not list it). Stale comment only.

#### Surprises

1. Every customer email shows a broken logo. `emailLogoUrl()` (packages/email/src/brand.ts:35) points at `https://app.brandbasket.in/email/email-logo.png`; that file exists only in apps/platform/public, and nginx sends `/email/` to apps/customer. Copy it into apps/customer/public/email/ (or add `/email/` to the split regex) before apps/platform/public is removed.
2. The staff "App errors" inbox no longer hears from the customer app. Browser capture, the server error hook, per-action `reportServerError` calls and the system-error alert are all absent in apps/customer (L-088, L-089). The `/api/error-reports` route there has no caller. Browser Sentry may also be off in production for both apps: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` is not in deploy/vps/brandbasket-platform-build.env (UNVERIFIED elsewhere).
3. The customer env template omits all GA4 and Meta conversion keys (L-079, L-080). The sign-up event is sent only from apps/customer, so it silently stops unless the VPS customer.env.production was filled by hand. The deploy check (release script :169-183) does not look for them.
4. Payload tenant creation was dropped in two places (setup redemption and first-payment confirmation), noted only by a `shortcut:` code comment (cust/features/entry/server/setup.ts:207).
5. `/robots.txt` and `/.well-known/security.txt` fall to apps/customer and 404.
6. The nginx brute-force guard in the cutover file covers `login|join|forgot-password|reset-password|restore-account` only. `/signup` (emails codes and creates accounts), `/setup`, `/affiliate/setup` and `/invite` are outside it. The app's own database limits still apply.
7. Plan 61 line 84 says /signup "keeps claim"; no code anywhere reads `claim`, and the old app already dropped it (PARITY.md:33).
8. Deep links are lost at sign-in in both apps (guards redirect to `/login` with no return path; only `/invite/...` is honoured, cust/features/entry/rules/account-entry.ts:40-43). A signed-out customer clicking a receipt, pay or request link lands on Home after signing in. Not a regression, but worth fixing before relying on email links.
9. `isUntrackedPath` omits `/invite/<token>` and `/affiliate/setup/<token>`, so those tokens can enter GTM and Clarity URLs after consent.
10. Three secrets must match across apps and are only warned about: `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` (sessions, blog preview signature, platform verifies), `LEADS_API_TOKEN` (ad `start` tokens), `APP_ORIGIN`. Sessions end at cutover if the first differs.
11. `/content-media/<id>` is served by a file inside app-host and reached through proxy.ts's `/app-host` rewrite. Delete the group wholesale and the nginx split path 404s. The customer app never uses that route, so the cleaner fix is to drop `content-media/` and `/api/content-media-uploads` from the nginx split first.
12. Several permanent (308) redirects (`/support/<id>`, `/plan`, `/services`, `/personalize`, `/join`, `/billing/pay`) are cached by browsers; changing their targets later will not reach people who already followed them.
13. The old receipt, billing and pay pages treated "unpaid" differently (sent to `/onboarding/review`); the new guard sends unpaid accounts to `/checkout` (cust/lib/guard-rules.ts:63). Onboarding step deep links (`/onboarding/business` etc.) now land on the checklist without opening the step.
