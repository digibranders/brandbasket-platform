Data storage and privacy compliance

How your business and customer information is protected under Indian data protection principles.

Enquiry retention period: Customer website form inquiries are retained for 24 months to maintain customer order history, after which they are archived or deleted per your instruction.

Clear 30-day restore period policy
When you initiate account deletion:
  • Your website sweetcrust.in will be taken offline immediately.
  • Automated posting to your connected Instagram and Facebook channels will stop immediately.
  • Your recurring Growth Plan subscription of Rs 4,999 a month will be cancelled and will not renew.
  • You have a 30-day restore period: We keep your website backups, design files, and customer enquiry archives intact for 30 calendar days. If you change your mind within 30 days, simply sign in to restore your account and resume services instantly.
  • After 30 days: All data, media assets, and server backups are erased permanently and cannot be recovered by our team or anyone else.
