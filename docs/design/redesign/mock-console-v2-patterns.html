Console v2 patterns

The addendum from section 4 of the console redesign plan, as a living page. Eight patterns, each in every state: default, hover or focus, loading, empty, error, selected and disabled. Switch the width above to see the 390 layout. Everything under "Try it" is live: a real table, panel, toast and command bar you can drive from the keyboard.

  • Rows are 44px, buttons 36px (44px at 390), focus is a 2px ink ring, motion is off under reduced motion.
  • Gold is kept to the selected-row wash and one marker. Specimens are not product screens, so the budget of three per viewport applies when they are placed on one.
  • Names are placeholders: <business>. Every column and word traces to a field named in the plan (section 3).
  • Icons are inline SVG placeholders. The build uses the Phosphor set, regular weight.

RowActions

One visible verb plus a three dot menu. The menu holds the less common actions as plain verbs, destructive ones last and red, never more than five items. The same menu opens on right click. The verb is the row's single primary; the menu is never a second primary.

SidePanel

An overlay layer, not a second raised surface: the list stays the focal card. 480px from the right at 1100px and up, a full sheet below, 240ms slide, scrim only on phone. The header names the object, the primary sits sticky in the foot, the URL carries ?open=. Closing returns focus to the row that opened it.

KeyDoes
Enter on a rowOpens the panel. Focus moves to the panel heading; the list behind stays scrollable on wide screens.
EscCloses the panel, focus returns to the opening row. If a dialog or menu is above it, closes that first.
j kWith the panel open and focus outside any field, step to the next or previous row and swap the panel content.
eRuns the panel's primary (same verb as the row). Confirms first when it emails the customer.
TabCycles inside the panel on phone (scrim present). On wide screens focus may leave to the list; the panel is a complementary region, not a modal.

Toast

Bottom left, ink background, white text. 10 seconds when it carries Undo, 4 seconds otherwise, announced politely. Errors do not time out. The timer pauses on hover and focus. Undo only appears where no customer email was sent; anything that emailed already left, and is confirmed before, not undone after.

Stack rules
  • At most three visible; older ones collapse into the newest's "and 2 more".
  • Stack sits bottom left on wide screens, above the bottom edge full width on phone.
  • Enter from below, 8px rise, 220ms. No exit animation beyond a fade.
  • Never covers a SidePanel foot or BulkBar on phone: it lifts above them.

CommandBar

One overlay for search, go to and actions, opened with Ctrl K or Cmd K (native <dialog>, as today's global-search.tsx). Groups: Recent, Go to, Actions, then results by kind (client, enquiry, request, invoice, payment, email, 8 per kind). Prefix > shows actions only, # searches request references. Recent items live in the browser only.

Ctrl EnterOpens the result in a new tab.
EscClears the text, then closes. Focus returns to what had it.
