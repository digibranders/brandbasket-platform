## What changed

- Stepper: Kickoff, Build, Preview, Launch, Live (shared `Stepper`), above the Next step card whenever the client has a project. Done steps are sage, the current step is gold. On hold and Cancelled show no current step and say so in words, because the stage the build stopped at is not recorded (the mock hard codes Build).
- 14 stages (`lib/website-stage.ts`): waiting for kickoff, call ended and not confirmed, ready, building, building late, preview sent, preview silent, changes requested, approved, approved with address check failing, live, live with domain move asked, on hold, cancelled. The stage drives the stepper, the Domain and hosting fold and the kickoff card. The existing Next step card, facts row, preview versions and feedback folds are kept and unchanged in behaviour.
- Kickoff has one place to act: the kickoff prompt (`TodayKickoffPrompt`, imported, not edited). The Website card (call ended, not confirmed) and the Overview exception strip and Next step card show a "Call completed" button that only opens it. `KickoffCallSection` and the Calls surfaces stay state only; no second kickoff button exists. This closes CR-05 open question 1. While the call has not ended the card shows the state only.
- Confirms (plan 3.2, shared `ConfirmCopyDialog`, "Keep" cancels, mounted only while open): send preview, send updated preview, resend preview email, resend launch email, mark live, "Ready for customer's DNS" (both places), put on hold, resume. Cancel build and add a free round already had a confirm and are untouched. Nothing about the forms changed: same action, same fields, same `formNoValidate` on the preview button; the confirm only intercepts the click and submits the form with the same button as submitter.
- Domain and hosting: one fold around the existing Move to their own domain and Domain sections (domain facts, free address, move). It opens itself when the stage is approved, the address check is failing, or a move was asked, so `#domain` links land on visible content. The hosting form stays in Site details (carried over as the mock says).
- Builder select removed from `ProjectControls` (no assignee UI, ruling D12). `assignBuilderAction` and its tests are untouched.
- States: loading (`website/loading.tsx`), error (`website/error.tsx`, Try again), empty (no project and no website service says "No website service. ..."; no project with the service bought still says "No project yet."), success (existing notices).
- Preview silent: the card shows "No reply for N days" from 5 days (ruling 15; same constant as the Overview strip).

## New wording for owner approval

Confirms. Send the preview: "The customer is emailed this link and the build moves to Awaiting client review." Send the updated preview: "The customer is emailed the updated preview and the build moves to Awaiting client review." Resend the preview email: "The customer is emailed the preview link again. The stage does not change." Resend the launch email: "The customer is emailed their launch details again." Mark this website live: "The website is marked live and the customer is emailed their website address." Ask the customer to connect their domain: "The customer is emailed, and the DNS records show on their dashboard." (primary "Email the customer"). Put this build on hold: "The build pauses and the customer sees your reason exactly as you typed it. Their plan and billing do not change." Resume this build: "The build starts again from the stage it was in, and the customer sees that stage again."

Cards. "The call ended <day>, <time>. The 48 hour first preview clock has not started. After 24 hours the build starts on its own, labelled Started without call confirmation." "Kickoff call booked for <day>, <time>. Nothing is needed from us until the call ends." "No kickoff call is booked yet. Nothing is needed from us until the customer books one." "No reply for <n> days". "No website service. Their website build appears here once they have paid for Website Management." Stepper: "On hold. No stage is current until the build resumes." "Cancelled. No stage is current." Fold title "Domain and hosting".
