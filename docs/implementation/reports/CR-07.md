# CR-07: Services tab and Money tab

Branch `cr/07-services-money`, from `origin/development`. Plan sections S8, S9, 5.3 CR-07. Rulings 10, 12, 13. Not verified in a browser.

## What changed

- Services tab (`/services`): one card per service the client has (Website, Social media, Paid Ads, Monitoring) with its status chip and where it is run. Website links to its tab. Social says staff do no Social work (D16). Monitoring has no screen (D15). A paid and not yet contacted service links to Money `#add-ons`.
- Paid Ads figures work from the Paid Ads card: the existing form and `saveNumbersAction`, with the three ad fields only (ad spend, ad budget, enquiries from ads) and the last three months in three ad columns. The Social fields and the Local SEO + AEO form are not shown (rulings 10, 13).
- OyeChats form (`ClientChatbots`, unchanged) lives below the cards when the website was bought (ruling 12). Its two reads fail on their own with "We could not load the chatbots. Nothing was changed." and Try again.
- A "Legacy manual tools" fold holds Posts and reports (`WorkSection`), Campaigns, Accounts and Updates for the customer, all unchanged (CD-5, CD-21). It opens by itself when one of their notices comes back.
- Money tab (`/billing`): payments table first (flagged payments, when there are any, above it), then the plan lines (`ClientAddOns`), Billing facts, billing details, tax invoices, extras. Figures, OyeChats, updates, campaigns, accounts and work are gone from it. No Package or Ad credit row (`packageFacts` null). Roles without the money capability still see the plan lines only, as before.
- Notices: the actions still redirect to `/billing?numbers=...` (no action changed). The Money page forwards any notice another tab owns to that tab, keeping the query; `NOTICE_TABS` now routes work, numbers, updates, chatbots, campaigns and accounts to Services.
- No migration. No server action, query, billing or access rule changed. Refund dialogs, `ClientPayments`, `ClientCharges`, billing details and every confirm dialog are untouched.

## Open questions

1. Saving the ad figures replaces that month's row, so the Social fields (posts, reach, followers, profile visits) a month already holds are cleared on the next save. Intended by ruling 10; confirm no one relies on them.
2. The Paid Ads card shows the figures when the business has bought Social or Paid Ads by the bought-services rule, which can differ slightly from the live-lines rule that decides the card exists. A card with no form means the two disagree.
3. The Legacy fold is always shown (the mock says only while the client has stored data). There is no read for stored data, so it was not added ("no data source, no element").
4. Social card has no channel, last post or failure (N7 not landed) and no link out to the social-auto dashboard (no source for the URL).
5. The figures and legacy sections read their own data; a failure there is caught by the route error page, not a per-card error. Only the chatbots reads have a per-panel error.
6. After an action the notice lands on Services via the Money page redirect (two hops); fragments such as `#numbers` survive the redirect in browsers.
7. Money tab loading is the workspace skeleton; the mock's "Nothing to pay yet" empty state is not added (the payments component keeps its own empty line).
