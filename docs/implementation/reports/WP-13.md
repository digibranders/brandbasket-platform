# WP-13 Implementation Report: Website Track Fixes, Kickoff Wording, and Preview Link

Package: WP-13 Website track fixes (Release 1 priority 4, steps 7.2 to 7.8)
Branch: `wp/13-website-track-fixes`
Date: 2026-11-14

## 1. Overview and What Changed

This package completes the website customer journey and staff queue handling to ensure the website track is self-explanatory and complete on both apps.

### (a) Preview-Ready Email and In-App Review Link
- Updated `packages/email/src/templates/preview-ready.ts` to accept `reviewUrl` (pointing to `/website` on the customer app).
- Updated email button to "Review your website" linking directly to `reviewUrl` where customers can approve the build or submit revision requests.
- Added explanatory copy explaining that actions live on the dashboard, and noting that direct email replies are still received and turned into change requests.
- Updated `apps/admin/lib/server/project-preview.ts` to supply `reviewUrl` (`new URL("/website", serverEnv().APP_ORIGIN).toString()`) when sending or resending preview emails.

### (b) Customer Status Lines and Kickoff Wording
- Aligned customer status lines and staff console notices with AGENT-RULES.md section 2 and journey steps 7.1 to 7.2:
  - Kickoff status wording: "Call completed" (never "held").
  - Before call: "Your kickoff call is booked for <time>".
  - After call time and before confirmation: "We will start your website right after your call".
  - After call completed: "Thanks for the call. We are getting your website started".
- Updated `apps/customer/src/features/home/rules.ts` (`websiteCopy` for `kickoff_unconfirmed` and `starting`).
- Updated `apps/customer/src/features/website/rules/progress.ts` (`progressView`).
- Updated `apps/customer/src/features/calls/components/kickoff-panel.tsx`.
- Updated `apps/admin/lib/call-form.ts` (`CALL_NOTICES.held` and kickoff row status).

### (c) Approved Previews and Revisions Due Dates
- Created working day SLA helpers in `packages/db/src/working-days.ts` and re-exported via `packages/shared/src/lib/working-days.ts`:
  - `workingDaysAfter(now: Date, days: number): Date`
  - `revisionDueAt(now: Date): Date` (2 working days SLA)
  - `approvedDueAt(now: Date): Date` (1 working day SLA)
- Updated `packages/db/src/queries/projects.ts`:
  - `requestProjectRevisions`: updates `status: "revisions"`, increments `revisionRoundsGranted`, and sets `slaDueAt: revisionDueAt(input.now)`. This prevents revisions from displaying stale late dates from the initial build.
  - `approveProjectPreview`: updates `status: "approved"` and sets `slaDueAt: approvedDueAt(input.now)`.
- Updated `apps/admin/lib/today-queue.ts`:
  - Added `approved` to `TodayKind`.
  - Added `approvedPreviews` to `TodayInput`.
  - Added "Launch" action label for approved items.
  - Configured icon `CheckCircle` in `apps/admin/components/today/needs-someone.tsx`.
- Updated `apps/admin/lib/server/today.ts` to surface approved preview projects from `sources.clients` as Today rows.

### (d) Domain Move Requests on Today
- Added `listDomainMoves(db: Database)` query in `packages/db/src/queries/domain-connect.ts` returning active non-primary domain requests with business details.
- Updated `apps/admin/lib/today-queue.ts`:
  - Added `domain` to `TodayKind`.
  - Added `domainMoves` to `TodayInput` with 24-hour wait limit before late.
  - Configured icon `Globe` in `apps/admin/components/today/needs-someone.tsx`.
- Updated `apps/admin/lib/server/today.ts` to query `listDomainMoves` and include domain moves in the Today queue.
