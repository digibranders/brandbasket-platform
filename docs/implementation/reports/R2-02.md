## 1. What changed for the customer or the team
Re-themed the customer Home screen and Needs your OK section according to `docs/design/redesign/mock-home.html` and `docs/design/redesign/05-PREMIUM-DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md`:
1. Two-column responsive layout:
   - Primary content column (8 columns desktop) hosting the focal card, service cards, and activity charts.
   - Secondary aside column (4 columns desktop) hosting Needs your OK list, kickoff notes, upcoming calendar, and included edit meters.
2. Focal `NextActionCard`:
   - Single focal element per screen with 20px radius, 3px rattan gold top border, 40px icon tile with rattan gold wash, elevated warm shadow, and primary action.
   - Applied across all journey states: unpaid invoice payment (`PaymentTask`), kickoff call booking and waiting (`KickoffPanel`), build progress tracker (`ProgressScreen`), and preview review (`ReviewScreen`).
3. `NeedsYourOkList`:
   - Section header with count badge, rows with 40px tile, item age in Mono, review button, and peaceful empty state with soft sage check icon.
   - Dual accessibility labeling preserving both existing test assertions and the redesigned visual identity.
4. Bell notification dropdown (`NotificationsBell`):
   - Re-themed with rattan gold dot trigger, 40px tiles, mono timestamps, "Needs your OK" tags, and direct Review action links.
5. Service cards:
   - Website, Social media, Search presence, and Reputation management cards using `ServiceCard` with status chips (Active with live green dot, Next step, Waiting, Idle).
6. Journey states covered:
   - New customer, unpaid invoice, kickoff unbooked/booked, in-build progress, review preview, active overview, and viewer roles.
7. Quality bar:
   - Uses only tokens from `src/styles/tokens.css` and shared patterns from `@brandbasket/shared/ui/patterns`.
   - Zero Tailwind arbitrary classes in customer features.
   - Warm brown-black shadows only. Exactly one focal element per screen. Rattan gold accent 3 times or fewer per viewport.
   - Zero em or en dashes in code, copy, and documentation.
