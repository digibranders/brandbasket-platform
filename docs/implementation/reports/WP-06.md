# WP-06: Database Role Check and Tenant Isolation Report

## 1. What Changed
- **Start-up Database Role Check**:
  - Implemented `assertCustomerAppRole` and `verifyStartupDatabaseRole` in `packages/db/src/scope.ts`.

## 5. Staff Query Findings Sample
We audited remaining staff-database (`getStaffDb`) query sites across the customer app codebase to verify whether tenant/business scoping is correctly enforced:

1. `apps/customer/src/features/calls/server/calls.ts`:
   - Purpose: Staff DB used for booking and syncing team calls with Cal.com integration.
   - Findings: Properly scoped to `session.businessId`. All lookups and inserts derive the tenant from the authenticated session context. No IDOR vulnerability.
2. `apps/customer/src/features/website/server/actions.ts`:
   - Purpose: Website publishing and domain verification.
   - Findings: Explicitly filters on `businessId` derived from `requireBusiness()`. No cross-tenant access.
3. `apps/customer/src/features/domain/server/actions.ts`:
   - Purpose: Domain ownership verification and primary domain assignments.
   - Findings: Scoped to `businessId`. Uniqueness checks prevent hijacking domains already claimed by other tenants.
4. `apps/customer/src/features/account/server/export-download.ts`:
   - Purpose: Customer account data export retrieval.
   - Findings: Validates user ID against session user and restricts export documents to the caller's business IDs.
5. `apps/customer/src/features/account/server/account-export.ts`:
   - Purpose: Generates ZIP archives of client business data.
   - Findings: Queries iterate specifically over the businesses associated with the authenticated user ID.
6. `apps/customer/src/features/team/server/actions.ts`:
   - Purpose: Staff invites, member additions, and role updates.
   - Findings: Every query constrains `businessId = session.businessId`. Prevents adding or modifying members of another business.
7. `apps/customer/src/features/team/server/loader.ts`:
   - Purpose: Loads roster of members for the active business.
   - Findings: Strictly filtered by `businessId`.
8. `apps/customer/src/features/billing/server/add-ons.ts`:
   - Purpose: Purchasing add-ons and generating open charges.
   - Findings: Staff connection used because charges table restricts inserts to staff/system role. Scoped strictly to `session.businessId`.
9. `apps/customer/src/app/api/webhooks/calcom/route.ts`:
   - Purpose: Ingestion webhook from Cal.com.
   - Findings: Uses webhook booking payload UID to lookup existing `team_calls` records. Updates are restricted to matching rows.
10. `apps/customer/src/app/api/webhooks/oyechats/route.ts`:
    - Purpose: Ingests chatbot lead messages.
    - Findings: Resolves tenant by chatbot token/secret, then creates enquiries scoped to the verified business ID.
11. `apps/customer/src/features/privacy/server/privacy-requests.ts`:
    - Purpose: Data deletion requests (GDPR / DPDP compliance).
    - Findings: Audited staff DB usage; verifies that privacy requests are logged with caller user ID.
12. `apps/customer/src/app/api/site/events/route.ts` & `site/info/route.ts`:
    - Purpose: Client website analytics ping and public metadata.
    - Findings: Resolves business from cryptographically verified site token or domain hostname before executing inserts/queries.

Conclusion: No cross-tenant data leaks were identified in the sampled staff-database query call sites. All operations derive `businessId` either from the verified session or cryptographic webhook/token signatures.

## 6. Open Questions and Architectural Notes
1. **Elevated Staff Connections in Customer App**:
   - `getStaffDb()` is utilized in specific customer app flows (e.g., creating charges, recording audit logs, ingesting webhooks) where `bb_app` lacks write permissions under PostgreSQL RLS policies.
   - *Recommendation for future milestone*: Consider moving write operations requiring elevated privileges behind an internal RPC / job queue (such as BullMQ or Cloud Tasks) rather than maintaining direct `bb_staff` database credentials within the customer-facing container.
