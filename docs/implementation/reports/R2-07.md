## 1. What changed

Re-themed the Settings family and the account pages to `docs/design/redesign/mock-settings.html` and `05-PREMIUM-DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md`. No behaviour change: actions, guards, loaders, redirects and Release 1 rules are untouched.

- Settings is one tabbed pattern: the shared `SettingsTabs` (page links, `aria-current`, client navigation through Next `Link`). Tabs: Profile, Sign-in and security, Notifications, Social media (only with the service, page untouched), Team (owner), Privacy and data, Delete account (owner, red tone).
- One focal card per tab (gold top edge, 20px radius, warm hero shadow): Profile, Change password, Email notifications, People with access, Your data, Latest updates (What's new). Delete account has the red-edged card. Other cards are quiet 14px cards.
- States: loading (`settings/loading.tsx` skeleton), empty (Team with only the owner), error with recovery (existing `PanelError` with Try again, kept per card), permission (a non-owner sees "Ask your owner to do this." in Privacy and data; Team and Delete still send non-owners Home as before), success (existing toasts).
- Account pages (sign in, forgot password, reset password, setup link, restore account, no-business, invite) now sit in one focal card. The sign-up page keeps its own card (R2-05).
- Invite page: a chip states the invitation (Invitation open, Right address, Expired, Already used, Withdrawn, Not valid, Different address) above the title, before any Join button. Setup link "This link no longer works." carries a "Link used or expired" chip.
- Release 1 behaviour kept: invitee lands in the inviter workspace, workspace switch dialog, safe return-to-link, unpaid access to Settings and Help (no guard or action changed).
