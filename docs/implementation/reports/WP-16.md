# WP-16 Implementation Report: Billing Exceptions, Self-Serve Card Change and Lapse Policy

Package: WP-16 Billing exceptions (Release 1 priority 7, steps 12.1 to 12.5, P1-3, D10)
Branch: `wp/16-billing-exceptions`
Date: 2026-11-14

## 1. Overview and What Changed

This package ensures that a failed renewal can be resolved directly by the customer without writing to staff or waiting for a manual link, surfaces past-due and lapsed accounts in the staff console Today queue, formalizes the payment lapse policy behind constants and console copy, and adds an automated reminder email before the first full-price charge after an introductory month.

### (a) Failed-Payment Email "Pay Now" Direct Link
- Updated `packages/shared/src/server/payment-emails.ts` so the "Pay now" link in `paymentFailedEmail` directs customers to the direct payment step (`PAY_STEP_PATH = "/billing/payment"`) instead of the general `/billing` overview.
- This allows customers receiving a failed renewal notice to jump straight to fixing their payment method without navigation loops.

### (b) Self-Serve Payment Method Change (Razorpay Hosted Flow)
- Replaced the legacy "request a secure link from staff" loop with direct provider-hosted payment method update links.
- Added `getPaymentMethodUpdateUrl(providerSubscriptionId: string): Promise<string>` to the `BillingProvider` interface (`packages/billing/src/provider.ts`).
- Implemented `getPaymentMethodUpdateUrl` in:
  - `packages/billing/src/razorpay-provider.ts` and `packages/billing/src/razorpay-sdk-adapter.ts`: queries Razorpay subscriptions API (`subscriptions.fetch(id)`) to retrieve Razorpay's native `short_url` for customer card/mandate update.
  - `packages/billing/src/fake-provider.ts`: generates deterministic mock provider update URLs for test environments.
- Added server query `getPaymentMethodUpdateUrlFor(customer)` in `apps/customer/src/features/billing/server/billing.ts`.
- Added server action `changePaymentMethodAction()` in `apps/customer/src/features/billing/actions.ts` returning `{ updateUrl }`.
- Rewrote `PaymentMethodRequest` in `apps/customer/src/features/billing/components/payment-method-request.tsx`:
  - When the customer clicks "Change payment method", an action fetches the provider update link and displays a direct dialog with a "Change on Razorpay" link opening in a secure new tab.
  - Retains a graceful fallback notice if the subscription is not active or hosted update is unavailable.

### (c) Today Rows for Past-Due and Lapsed Billing Statuses
- Extended `TodayInput` in `apps/admin/lib/today-queue.ts` to support optional `billing?: readonly TodayItem[]` items, integrated into the existing sort comparator (`sortTodayItems`).
- Updated `queueFrom` in `apps/admin/lib/server/today.ts` to surface billing exceptions for staff when `canSeeMoney` is true:
  - `billing:past_due:<businessId>`: for active subscriptions with status `past_due`. Action: "View billing", title: "Payment past due: <businessName>", detail: "Renewal payment failed", href: `/clients/<businessId>/billing`, sorted by `paidThroughAt`.
  - `billing:lapsed:<businessId>`: for lapsed accounts where payment has run out. Action: "View billing", title: "Plan lapsed: <businessName>", detail: "Plan has ended", href: `/clients/<businessId>/billing`, sorted by `paidThroughAt ?? createdAt`.

### (d) Payment Lapse Policy and Console Money Page Summary
- Created `packages/billing/src/lapse-policy.ts` holding the formal lapse policy constants:
  - `LAPSE_GRACE_PERIOD_DAYS = 7`: grace period during which service access remains available.
  - `LAPSE_HOSTING_PAUSE_DAYS = 14`: timeline at which hosted sites are paused.
  - `LAPSE_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS = 30`: timeline for which customer data is retained before erasure.
- Integrated `LAPSE_GRACE_PERIOD_DAYS` into `packages/billing/src/access.ts`:
  - Subscriptions with status `past_due` retain dashboard billing access for 7 days after `paidThroughAt`.
  - In `packages/billing/src/checkout.ts`, `decideCheckout` ensures customers in grace whose paid period ended are not blocked by "already-paid", enabling self-serve checkout reactivation.
- Integrated `LAPSE_HOSTING_PAUSE_DAYS` into `packages/billing/src/hosting-plan.ts`:
  - Added `paidThroughAt` to `HostedSiteState`.
  - Added pause reason `"within-lapse-window"` when an unpaid site is within the 14-day hosting pause window.
- Added a clear Lapse Policy summary card to the staff console Money page (`apps/admin/app/(protected)/payments/page.tsx`) rendering the exact policy terms without en or em dashes.

### (e) First Full-Price Charge Reminder Email
- Created email template `packages/email/src/templates/first-charge-reminder.ts` (`firstChargeReminderEmail`):
  - Informs the customer of their upcoming regular monthly charge date, plan amount, and billing settings link.
  - Strictly avoids en dashes and em dashes.
- Implemented `sendFirstChargeReminder` in `packages/shared/src/server/payment-emails.ts`:
  - Safe, idempotent delivery: checks `hasEmailContaining` for template `first-charge-reminder` matching the customer's billing URL since the subscription started.
  - Prevents duplicate reminder sends across background or worker invocations.
