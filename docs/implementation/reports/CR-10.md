## What changed for staff
- A failed payment or an overdue plan is never "All clear". The Needs a look tile adds payments and renewals, and equals the Money count in the rail (current month). If plans or flagged payments cannot be read, the tile says so and shows "?" instead of zero, and the lane says it could not read.
- No Package or Ad credit wording appears on these pages (none was found on them; `/ad-credit` is untouched, owned by CR-13).
- Refund dialog, tick box and wording are unchanged (`refund-dialogs.tsx` not touched). Nothing money-moving is bulk or undoable.
