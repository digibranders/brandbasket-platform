## 1. What changed for the customer or the team
- Re-themed the customer app from the previous blue (`#1E65E3`) and Geist fonts to the BrandBasket website brand:
  - Primary button: near-black (`#15161A`), hover `#2A2B31`, with white text (`#FFFFFF`) and inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`.
  - Accent: rattan gold (`#C48A47`), gold text (`#A46C2F`), gold wash (`#F4E6D3`), and gold deep (`#8A5524`).
  - Ground and surfaces: warm paper ground (`#F6F5F2`), clean white panels (`#FFFFFF`), warm sunken wells (`#EEECE7`), hairline dividers (`#E3E0D9`).
  - Meaning: sage (`#4F7A63`, wash `#E5EFE8`, text `#2F5A43`), error (`#B42318`, wash `#FDECEA`).
- Replaced Geist with Google Fonts via `next/font`:
  - `Red_Hat_Display` for page titles, headings, and hero tasks (`--font-red-hat-display`).
  - `Red_Hat_Text` for body copy, controls, buttons, and navigation (`--font-red-hat-text`).
  - `Red_Hat_Mono` for figures, dates, times, money, and identifiers (`--font-red-hat-mono`).
- Replaced cold/pure black shadows with warm brown-black elevations:
  - Resting cards: `1px solid var(--line)` and `0 1px 0 rgba(42,28,16,0.03)`.
  - Focal/hero cards and popovers: `0 1px 2px rgba(42,28,16,0.05), 0 12px 32px -12px rgba(42,28,16,0.14)`.
  - Dialogs: `0 24px 64px -16px rgba(42,28,16,0.28)` over `rgba(21,22,26,0.40)` scrim.
- Standardized radii:
  - Controls: 8px (`--r-control: 8px`).
  - Standard cards: 14px (`--r-panel: 14px`).
  - Focal/hero cards and dialogs: 20px (`--r-overlay: 20px`).
  - Chips and badges: full pill (`9999px`).
- Spacing rhythm on 4px base:
  - Card padding: 28px desktop, 20px mobile.
  - Page padding: 40px desktop, 16px mobile. Max content width: 1240px.
- Verified WCAG 2.2 AA contrast across all 49 token pairs; all pass with zero regressions.
