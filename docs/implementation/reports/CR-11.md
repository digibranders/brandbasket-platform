# CR-11 Compliance

Branch `cr/11-compliance`. One Compliance area, four tabs at the same URLs: Privacy requests, Breaches, Closures (`/leaving`), Access log.

## What changed

- Every tab shows the clock strip (the most overdue running clocks across privacy requests and breaches), under `SectionTabs`. A register that cannot be read is named in the strip; its clocks keep running.
- Privacy requests and Breaches: tiles, then a `FilterBar` (Open, Closed), a `DataTable` with the most overdue first, and a `SidePanel`. Logging a request or breach is a side panel opened by the one dark button, so an overdue item is never pushed down by a form. The old inline forms, italic titles, "Received · received" repeat and the repo path in the Breaches line are gone. "Take this" is no longer shown (one staff account); the action still accepts it.
- Closing a privacy request or breach keeps its `ConfirmDialog` with a consequence line. Restore and "Extend by 30 days" are on the Closures table, each behind a `ConfirmDialog`, using the existing `restoreClosureAction` and `extendClosureAction` unchanged.
- Closures: the list of accounts being closed (from `listOpenClosures` and `listClients`), then what people said. "Stayed" shows with no offer percent (ruling 9).
- Access log: compact `DataTable`, human action name with the stored id in Mono under it, grouped action filter. `STAFF_ACCESS_ACTIONS` gained the 27 actions the code writes but did not list (the 16 from the audit plus the templated `setup_call.*`, `change_request.*`, `report.hold` and `report.release`). Before, a filter on any of them was dropped by `parseAccessLogFilters`.
- All states: loading (existing route `loading.tsx`), empty, error (`ReadFailed` with Try again), success (the row and strip change after the action; see open questions).

## New wording for owner approval

- Clock strip: "Clocks that need you first", "No clocks are running.", "A privacy request or a breach starts a clock the moment it is logged.", "The privacy requests could not be read, so their clocks are not shown. They keep running."
- Notice: "1 request is due in under 72 hours." / "N requests are due in under 72 hours." and "Open it first after any overdue one."
- Close privacy request consequence: "The request is recorded as closed now, with the outcome and note you give. A closed request cannot be reopened, and nothing here can be deleted."
- Close breach consequence: "The breach is recorded as closed now. It stays in the register and nothing here can be deleted."
- Mark as sent now (new confirm): title "Record the CERT-In report as sent" (and Data Protection Board, People affected); consequence "The report is recorded as sent at this moment. Once recorded, the time cannot be changed."
- Restore: the existing line is reused. Extend: title "Give {business} 30 more days", consequence "The erase date moves 30 days later. Their data stays until then."
- Empty and error lines for each tab (see the pages), Closures empty "No account is being closed."

## Open questions

1. Red under 72 hours. The brief and plan S18 say red under 72 hours; the approved mock notes (decision 4) and design language 8b rule 6 say red only when late, with a neutral bold notice. I followed the brief. One line in `components/compliance/clock-chip.tsx` (`soon: "failed"` to `"idle"`) switches it.
2. Success feedback. The actions redirect to a bare list URL, so there is no toast. After Restore or Extend the redirect lands on the client's People page and its notice is the confirmation. A toast needs the actions to redirect with a result, which is a behaviour change.
3. Step marks have no Undo: no reverse action exists (the reported times are kept by design).
4. "Mark as sent now" on breach reports now asks for a confirm because the time cannot be changed. This is new and beyond the plan's list; remove it if the owner wants one click in an emergency.
5. No Compliance rail count: adding it means changing two existing assertions (`console-rail.test.ts` "no count on Compliance", exact equality in `rail-counts.test.ts`). Today clock rows belong to the Today package.
6. Not built: client filter by business name (the Client id field remains), tab counts.
7. The Access log keeps its select and date filter form rather than `FilterBar`, because its filters are selects and dates, not pills.
8. Closures are open to every staff member as before; the strip, Restore and Extend only show with the oversight capability.
9. Clocks are computed when the page renders and do not tick on a page left open.
