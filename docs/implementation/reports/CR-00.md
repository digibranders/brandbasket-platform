# CR-00 report: console design docs and tokens

Branch `cr/00-console-docs`, from `origin/docs/console-rulings` (PR #226). Docs and tokens only, no application code.

## Changed

1. `docs/design/redesign/05-PREMIUM-DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md`: new section 8b (console patterns). Covers the settled conflicts (44px rows, 36px compact only for Invoices and Access log, overlays are not raised surfaces and carry the popover shadow, one primary with the panel foot carrying it when a panel is open, bordered white secondary buttons on white surfaces, confirmation dialog rule), the status vocabulary, the gold budget, the rail colour `#23242A`, DataTable, RowActions, SidePanel, FilterBar, InlineEdit, Toast with undo (4 s plain, 10 s undo, errors persist), BulkBar, CommandBar, the keyboard map (rulings 7 and 19) and the confirm and undo table. Sections 3 and 8 point to it.
2. `apps/admin/DESIGN.md`: rewritten to follow the language file. Surfaces, Lists, Status, Palette and Not allowed updated; new Confirm and undo section; dialogs and loading lines corrected. The old bans on modals for confirmation, outlined secondary buttons and shadows other than the raised surface are replaced by the narrower rules.
3. `packages/shared/src/styles/theme.css`: console-scoped tokens `--color-sage-text` (#3c6150), `--color-rattan-soft` (#fbf4ea), `--color-row-hover` (#faf9f6), `--shadow-popover`. The sage wash and error wash already existed (`--color-sage-wash`, `--color-error-wash`), so they were not added again.
4. `packages/shared/src/styles/theme-console.test.ts`: new. Checks the tokens are in the console block, text on wash is 4.5:1 or better, and the popover shadow equals the hero shadow.

## Contrast

Sage text on sage wash 5.9, on white 7.0. Error on error wash 5.75. Ink-2 on the soft gold wash 7.4. Ink-2 on row hover is above 7. The sage fill `#4F7A63` on its wash is 4.15, so it is a mark colour only, never text (noted in the theme comment).
