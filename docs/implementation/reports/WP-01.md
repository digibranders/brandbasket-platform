# WP-01: First Payment Recovery Report

## 1. What Changed
- **First Payment Recovery Job & Route**:
  - Updated the recurring first payment check in `apps/customer/src/platform/lib/server/first-payment-check.ts` and `apps/customer/src/app/api/internal/check-first-payments/route.ts` to inspect all pending first checkouts older than the 10-minute settle window, covering both standard plan checkouts and offer checkouts.
  - Queried Razorpay/provider for captured first payments and recorded payment method (`method`, `card_id`, `bank`, `wallet`, `vpa`) on recovery instead of storing `null`.
  - Processed recovered payments through the idempotent webhook handler (`handleRazorpayWebhookWithPayload`), ensuring safe concurrent and duplicate delivery handling.
  - Maintained amount mismatch safety: flagged mismatches are recorded as attention-required and subscriptions are not activated.
- **Systemd Service & Installer**:
  - Updated `deploy/vps/install.sh` to not only copy the `brandbasket-first-payment-check.timer` and `.service`, but explicitly enable and start the timer: `systemctl enable --now brandbasket-first-payment-check.timer`.
  - Verified systemd unit definitions in `deploy/vps/systemd/brandbasket-first-payment-check.service` and `.timer`.
