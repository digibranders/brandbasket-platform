# WP-01: First Payment Recovery Report

## 1. What Changed
- **First Payment Recovery Job and Route**:
  - Updated the recurring first payment check in `apps/customer/src/platform/lib/server/first-payment-check.ts` and `apps/customer/src/app/api/internal/check-first-payments/route.ts` to inspect all pending first checkouts older than the 10-minute settle window, covering both standard plan checkouts and offer checkouts.
  - Queried Razorpay/provider for captured first payments and recorded payment method (`method`, `cardNetwork`, `cardLast4`, `cardType`, `cardIssuer`, `upiVpa`) on recovery instead of storing `null`.
  - Processed recovered payments through the idempotent webhook handler (`recordChargeFromRazorpay` / `recordUpfrontFromRazorpay`), ensuring safe concurrent and duplicate delivery handling.
  - Maintained amount mismatch safety: flagged mismatches are recorded as attention-required and subscriptions are not activated.
  - Retained existing page-load recovery in `apps/customer/src/features/checkout/loader.ts`.
- **Systemd Service and Installer**:
  - Updated `deploy/vps/install.sh` to not only copy the `brandbasket-first-payment-check.timer` and `.service`, but explicitly enable and start the timer when `$SRV/current-customer` exists: `systemctl enable --now brandbasket-first-payment-check.timer`.
  - Updated systemd unit comments and descriptions in `deploy/vps/systemd/brandbasket-first-payment-check.service` and `.timer` to reflect ordinary checkout recovery.
