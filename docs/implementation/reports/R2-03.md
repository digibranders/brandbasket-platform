   * Integrated shared ErrorState pattern with warm alert styling and clear troubleshooting instructions when domain status is misconfigured.
   * Added StatusChip for domain states ("Connected" with dot, "DNS Error").
6. Sub-page navigation tabs visible from day one:
   * Overview, Pages, Blog, and Domain tabs are accessible right from onboarding and kickoff, ensuring transparent visibility into upcoming deliverables.
